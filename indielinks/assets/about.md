Indielinks is
[del.icio.us](https://web.archive.org/web/20080906021806/http://delicious.com/)
for the Fediverse: bookmark the web, follow people whose taste you
trust, and find content without a platform deciding what you see.

Social bookmarking used to be a means of content discovery; save
links, follow people, see what they were reading. Indielinks is social
bookmarking over ActivityPub. You can save & tag links; when you make
them public, your links and any notes you make about them will show-up
in your followers' timelines on indielinks, Mastodon, or any other
ActivityPub app.

[indielinks.sh](https://indielinks.sh) is the first indielinks
instance. I'm running it on a limited, "friends & family" basis for
now.
